## Production change

The Tax Engine resolves the next preferential band through a full additional-$1 long-term-gain counterfactual, including any increase in calculated taxable Social Security. It preserves selected calculation options and supplied taxable-benefit facts, and counts Schedule D income only once. The adapter forwards the result and the UI formats it without a zero-rate fallback. Existing marginalPreferentialRate semantics are preserved. Rule metadata increments to 1.1.0 for the expanded output contract.

Independent review correction: at $41,729.72 wages and $30,000 Social Security (2026 single), taxable income starts at $49,449.98. An added $1 gain produces $49,451.83 taxable income and $0.25 additional modeled federal tax, including $0.15 preferential tax. The cue is 15%, not the total marginal federal rate. Existing current-input and projected-input cases now cover this boundary and saved-input immutability; the existing browser campaign enters it through visible Tax controls. These extensions add no test cases or suites beyond the original F09 candidate.
